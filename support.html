Support

Need help with one of our apps? Email hello@mondara.org and tell us which app, your device and what happened.

Restoring purchases

In greed.io, open the MENU and choose RESTORE PURCHASES. Purchases are tied to your Apple ID, so sign in with the same one you bought with.

Refunds

Apple handles refunds for App Store purchases. You can request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.
