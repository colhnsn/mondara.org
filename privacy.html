Privacy Policy

Last updated October 8, 2026

This policy covers the apps Motherboard Systems LLC publishes, including greed.io, and this website.

What our apps collect

Nothing. Our apps have no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and they don't send your data to us or to anyone else.

Where your data stays

Your progress and settings are saved on your device, and in your device's backups (iCloud or your computer) if you make them. Deleting the app erases them from the device. Older backups keep their copy until they are replaced.

Purchases

In-app purchases go through Apple's App Store, and Apple handles the payment. We never see your payment details. Apple's own privacy policy covers that part.

Children

Since we collect no personal information from anyone, we collect none from children either.

This website

This site uses no cookies, analytics or trackers. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, for security.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update the date above. Questions go to hello@mondara.org.
